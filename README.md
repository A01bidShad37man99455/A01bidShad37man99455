# Hi there! 👋

I'm **Md. Shadman Abid**, welcome to my GitHub profile! Here, you can find various source codes related to the applications of deep learning, deep reinforcement learning, and optimization techniques in sustainable energy systems. 

## Background
I am currently employed as a **Research Assistant** at the **Nanotechnology Research Center, Sultan Qaboos University (SQU)**, where I am involved in multiple projects, including a **vision-informed multi-modal AI framework for active distribution network scheduling**, **AI-driven power grid path prediction**, and **deep learning-based geospatial mapping for energy distribution networks**. Previously, I worked at the **Sustainable Energy Research Center, SQU**, where I developed **robust optimization and reinforcement learning-based models for EV charging infrastructure planning**, with a focus on the **techno-economic, environmental, and operational aspects of microgrid management**. Alongside my research, I also served as a **part-time lecturer** in the **Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE)** at **Sonargaon University, Dhaka, Bangladesh**. I graduated in **2022** with a **B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE)** from the **Islamic University of Technology (IUT), Gazipur, Bangladesh**. My research interests include **artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, and optimization**, particularly their applications in **sustainable energy systems, intelligent infrastructure planning, and data-driven decision-making**. My long-term goal is to contribute to the development of **intelligent cyber-physical systems** that seamlessly integrate **AI-driven learning methods** with **large-scale infrastructure applications**.
